Strategy

Story first. Doll later. Trust always.

Use this page before drafting. Every post should serve the pre-launch principle and speak to both the child and the parent without over-claiming.

Positioning

Category frame

A story-first companion universe where children befriend brave Indian heroines before they own the doll.

One-line promise

A friend who helps every child find the hero within.

Working positioning

Maitri creates story-rich Indian companion dolls that help children discover courage, kindness, curiosity, and leadership through the lives of remarkable women from India’s past and present.

Pre-launch principle

Fall in love with the characters long before the doll appears. Content should make Manu feel like a friend — not a distant queen or a product shot.

Child wins

  • Story emotion
  • Friendship and adventure
  • Repeatable play ideas
  • “What would Manu do?” moments

Parent wins

  • Representation that feels real
  • Values without lectures
  • Conversation prompts
  • Calm cultural care

First companion

  • Manu — inspired by Rani Laxmibai of Jhansi
  • Core value: courage with tenderness
  • Childhood in Bithoor; later Jhansi legacy kept distinct
  • Hot jalebis, Badal, asking “why”

Audience

Primary buyer

Urban and semi-urban Indian parents of children roughly ages 5–10 who want culturally rooted, meaningful, high-quality play.

Also in the circle

Grandparents and NRI families seeking Indian identity gifts; later, schools, libraries, and cultural communities — only after character love is established.

Claim guardrails

Bake these into every draft’s review notes.

Do say

  • Manu is a friend children can grow with through story and play.
  • Courage can look like asking why, trying again, or staying kind.
  • Stories are inspired by remarkable Indian women — keep sensitivity notes.
  • Invite waitlist interest without promising ship dates.

Don’t say (yet)

  • Safety, materials, compliance, or manufacturing guarantees.
  • Exact preorder, production, or delivery timelines.
  • Contested historical details presented as settled fact.
  • Violence or battle as the main aspiration of courage.

Draft checklist

Every content block in this studio should include:

  • Audience — child, parent, or both
  • Platform — Instagram, Shorts/Reels, newsletter, etc.
  • Hook — first line or first 2 seconds
  • Copy / script
  • Visual / motif — which brand icon and framing
  • Review notes — claims check + tone check
