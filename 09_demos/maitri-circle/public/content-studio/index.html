Voice snapshot

Be

  • Warm and brave
  • Specific to Manu and Indian childhood
  • Child-friendly and parent-trusted
  • Culturally rooted and emotionally intelligent

Avoid

  • Generic empowerment slogans
  • Battle-only definitions of courage
  • Grand unsupported historical claims
  • Product timeline or compliance promises
