Fifteen to thirty seconds of friendship, courage, and a clear pause.

Six short scripts. Keep on-screen text short. Voice warm. End with a question or soft invite — not a hard sell.

Audience: both Platform: Shorts / Reels · ~20s Pillar: Friendship · 26

Hook (0–2s)

On-screen: “Meet Manu as a friend.”

Beats

0–2s Hook text + friendship clover motif 2–8s VO: “Before the big stories adults tell, Manu was a girl who loved jalebis and asked hard questions.” 8–14s VO: “Her courage looks like friendship — especially with a horse named Badal.” 14–20s On-screen question: “Who would you introduce Manu to?” Soft end card with motif

VO intent

Intimate, smiling, never theatrical battle energy.
