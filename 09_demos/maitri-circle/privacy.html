Privacy Policy

This starter policy describes, in plain language, how Maitri Circle may handle information when you visit this family-facing story and companion website.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. Information you choose to share

You may be able to share information such as your name and email address when you choose to join a waitlist, send a message, or use another form on the website. We only ask for information that is reasonably needed for that particular activity.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting environment or technical services supporting Maitri Circle may receive basic information such as browser type, device type, approximate access time, and pages requested. This information may be used to keep the website secure, available, and functioning properly.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide a requested service, respond to a message, manage a waitlist, improve the website, understand basic website performance, prevent misuse, and meet applicable operational requirements. We do not make promises here about services or uses that have not yet been enabled.

Maitri Circle is intended to be explored with a parent or responsible adult. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly. If an adult believes a child has shared personal information with us, they should use the official contact route made available on the website so the matter can be reviewed.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or its hosting and embedded services may use essential cookies or similar technical tools to deliver pages, remember necessary preferences, protect forms, or understand basic usage. Any optional analytics or marketing tools should be described here before they are enabled.

6. Sharing information

Information may be processed by service providers that help host, secure, operate, or support the website, and may be disclosed where required by law or needed to protect the website and its users. We do not claim that the website currently uses any particular provider beyond what is visible in the live implementation.

7. Retention and security

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required. Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and questions

Depending on where you live and the context of your interaction, you may be able to ask what information is held about you, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw from a voluntary list. Use the official contact route made available on the website or through the communication channel where you received this link. Specific contact details and jurisdictional rights are intentionally not stated until confirmed.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as Maitri Circle grows or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page, with the updated date shown above.
