## What exists inside each package

### 1. Maitri Chief Of Staff

Existing behaviour:

- turns notes into a strategic read;
- records decisions made;
- identifies open questions;
- identifies risks and missing evidence;
- recommends next actions;
- avoids inventing founder decisions.

Best first demonstration:

`Summarize Maitri's current foundation direction for Shwetika in one page, with decisions made, open questions, and next actions.`

### 2. Manu Story And Product Editor

Existing behaviour:

- reviews child relatability;
- reviews parent trust;
- flags historical-care issues;
- checks first-box alignment;
- protects the tender-courage direction;
- flags safety, manufacturing, historical, and founder-review needs.

Best first demonstration:

`Review the current Manu 32-page storybook blueprint and tell me what needs to be decided before drafting page-by-page copy.`

### 3. Maitri Content Producer

Existing behaviour:

- creates platform-specific content drafts;
- defines audience and hook;
- provides copy or script;
- provides visual direction;
- provides review notes;
- avoids unsupported product, safety, compliance, production, and preorder claims.

Best first demonstration:

`Create three Instagram carousel ideas and three YouTube Shorts scripts for introducing Manu without making preorder or product claims.`

### 4. Maitri Signal Analyst

Existing behaviour:

- separates observed evidence from assumptions;
- identifies strong and weak signals;
- identifies repeated objections;
- recommends the next decision;
- states what should not happen yet;
- asks for missing data rather than guessing.

Best first demonstration:

`Using the monthly signal report template, tell me what data I need to collect this month before making any preorder decision.`

## Recommended presentation order

1. Chief Of Staff - show how a meeting becomes decisions and next actions.
2. Story And Product Editor - show how Manu material is reviewed safely.
3. Content Producer - show how one approved story becomes social and newsletter drafts.
4. Signal Analyst - show how published response becomes a next decision.

This order demonstrates a complete operating loop:

`Decide -> Review source -> Create derivatives -> Measure -> Decide again`

## Scope boundary

These agents provide guided ideation, drafting, structuring, review support, and analysis. They do not independently approve claims, publish content, make product commitments, replace historical or professional review, or operate external accounts without explicit authority and supported connections.
