# Manu & Badal - manuscript proof

This package turns the production brief into a 32-page landscape book manuscript using the Manu content already established in the Maitri source.

Files:

- `manu-and-badal-interior-manuscript-proof.pdf` - 32 individual interior pages at 303 x 216 mm (297 x 210 mm trim plus 3 mm bleed).
- `manu-and-badal-spread-preview.pdf` - 16 continuous landscape spreads for creative review. The gutter contains background scenery only; reading text remains on the right-hand page.
- `manu-and-badal-cover-concept.pdf` - front-cover direction at trimmed A4 landscape size.

Status: creative manuscript proof, not production artwork. The supplied source images are approximately 880 to 1,615 pixels wide and therefore do not meet the brief's 300 ppi at final placed size. Before printing, replace every placed image with licensed final artwork at production resolution; get the printer's ICC profile and final cover template; then export the final CMYK PDF/X-4 and obtain a physical proof.

The brief keeps activity pages in the bound book but specifies stickers as separate box inserts. This manuscript follows that direction and does not place stickers in the interior.
