# Maitri Foundation Retainer - 90-Day Status and Handover Report

## Executive summary

The Maitri Foundation Retainer began on 1 June 2026 to establish a practical pre-launch foundation across brand direction, website and waitlist support, character development, content workflows, AI-assisted systems, and early demand validation.

Across June, July, and August 2026, the engagement produced a substantial body of strategic, digital, editorial, story, product, and workflow infrastructure. The work completed is materially broader than a standalone website assignment. It includes the public-facing Maitri experience as well as the internal systems required to create, review, reuse, and learn from future content.

The foundation is now sufficiently developed to move into a more visible operating phase. The immediate priority is not further expansion of the foundation. It is to complete handover, approve a focused content direction, begin a measured publishing rhythm, and convert website and content response into a monthly decision report.

> The current position is best described as a strong foundation with an incomplete activation and handover phase.

This report documents the work completed, explains its practical value, identifies work that extended beyond the light retainer scope, and sets out the decisions required for the next phase.

## 1. Engagement basis

The signed agreement defines the engagement as a monthly Digital Growth, AI Systems, and Pre-Launch Brand Building Retainer. Its purpose includes:

- digital growth and pre-launch brand building;
- content workflows and social media direction;
- website and waitlist support;
- character universe development;
- basic AI workflows for content, newsletters, story expansion, and repurposing;
- beta-reader and parent-feedback foundations;
- monthly priorities, progress visibility, and shared decisions.

The agreement is not structured as a fixed-price website completion project or as fixed hourly availability. It is a monthly retainer based on agreed scope, deliverables, and project priorities.

The original strategic proposal also defines Kramaniti's role across three connected layers:

| Layer | Purpose for Maitri |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy | Clarify positioning, audience, priorities, roadmap, decisions, and launch posture. |
| Systems | Build the website, waitlist, AI workflows, content pipeline, review process, and feedback structure. |
| Content | Develop story-led social, newsletter, parent, founder, and character material that can be reviewed and distributed. |

## 10. Conclusion

The first three months created a credible and unusually broad foundation across strategy, website, story, content, AI workflows, and validation infrastructure. The amount and complexity of work completed are consistent with a foundation-building engagement rather than a standalone website assignment.

The next measure of progress should be different. Success should now be assessed through adoption and evidence:

- Shwetika can use the handed-over systems;
- approved content is published consistently;
- the waitlist and feedback loop produce usable signals;
- monthly decisions are made from those signals;
- scope, approvals, progress, and billing remain visible in writing.

This transition will allow Maitri to receive practical operating value from the foundation already created without continuing to expand invisible infrastructure.
