# Maitri Logo Round 2 Direction

## Rejected territory

Do not continue the standalone rounded or ribbon-built `M` badge territory. The first round felt too close to familiar McDonald's, Gmail, Myntra, and generic consumer-brand constructions.

## New principle

Make the full name `Maitri` the primary identity. Companionship should emerge from a proprietary relationship inside the wordmark rather than from a generic icon placed beside it.

## Concepts

1. **Friendship Thread** — two hand-drawn strokes meet inside the initial `M`; one continues through the word as a quiet thread.
2. **Shared Chapter** — two page-edge forms share one central spine inside the `M`, without becoming an envelope or literal book icon.
3. **Paired-i Companions** — the two `i` dots in `Maitri` become the paired companion gesture and are linked by a restrained story thread.
4. **Two Voices** — two quotation-like strokes meet inside the initial `M`, expressing conversation and companionship without a speech-bubble icon.

All concepts should preserve the exact spelling `Maitri`, the rose/plum/amber website palette, an Outfit-compatible rounded humanist tone, one-colour viability, and reproduction at small sizes.
