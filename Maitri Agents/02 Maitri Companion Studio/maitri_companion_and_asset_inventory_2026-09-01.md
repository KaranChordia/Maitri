## Required creation packet for a new or expanded companion

Before public copy, a public route, or final visual production, prepare:

1. Founder brief: who the companion is, why now, intended child age, and the distinct value role.
2. Historical/research source list: verified facts, citation-needed claims, sensitive details, and what must be avoided.
3. Character bible: use the uploaded template and mark review status.
4. Story direction: child lens, emotional arc, one first story premise, parent takeaway, and clear inspired-fiction boundary.
5. Visual brief: child age depiction, clothing and regional cues, pose/expression, palette, composition, and asset status.
6. Asset checklist: website-card portrait, route hero, story scenes, activities, doll/product references if approved, alt text, and filenames.
7. Public-copy pack: approved reveal hook, parent note, current-stage CTA, and prohibited claims.
8. Review record: historical, child-language, product, visual, founder, and publishing approvals.

## Operating boundary

A generated image, placeholder route, or older website preview does not establish biography, a historical claim, a product detail, a launch commitment, or approval to publish. A companion is ready for outward-facing development only when its character bible and required reviews are recorded.
