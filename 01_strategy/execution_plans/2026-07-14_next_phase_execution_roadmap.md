# Maitri Next-Phase Execution Roadmap

Date: 2026-07-14  
Status: Active first execution cycle  
Primary approval owner: Shwetika / founder

## Purpose

Turn the approved next-phase execution brief into a controlled sequence of work that strengthens the current Maitri foundation without creating a speculative redesign or expanding the product story ahead of evidence.

The phase is successful when Maitri operates as one coherent system:

- Maitri Circle is the clear story-first umbrella.
- Manu is the first complete companion and first-box proof.
- The website explains the promise before asking families to join the waitlist.
- Brand, storybook, and content decisions move through explicit founder gates.
- Public claims remain limited to approved and verifiable facts.

## Source-of-truth order

Use this hierarchy whenever sources disagree:

1. Confirmed founder decisions recorded in the decision register.
2. Repository governance and current strategy documents.
3. Approved character, first-box, and storybook source files.
4. The active Maitri Circle implementation and its local `AGENTS.md` instructions.
5. Supplied samples, screenshots, recordings, and generated concepts as reference-only inputs.

The supplied Manu sample HTML and screen capture are information-architecture references. They may inform content sequence, modular storytelling, and interaction patterns. They do not override Maitri's existing visual system and are not evidence for product, historical, manufacturing, pricing, availability, demand, safety, or delivery claims.

Exit gate: Shwetika selects a production route after reviewing samples, trade-offs, budget range, and supplier inputs.

- Inspect active implementation and repository state.
- Locate all supplied reference inputs.

### Phase 2: Implement and research

- Deliver the confirmed website slice.

### Phase 3: Select and prepare

- Refine the selected website and identity direction.
- Produce visiting-card artwork after identity selection.
- Turn storybook evidence into supplier-ready specifications.
- Convert approved content into reusable publishing templates.

## Decision discipline

Every decision is classified as one of:

- **Confirmed:** implementation may proceed.
- **Open:** options may be prepared, but no final claim or production commitment may be made.
- **Assumption:** must be verified before it affects public copy, cost, safety, history, or delivery.
- **Reference-only:** may inform structure or exploration but cannot override the source-of-truth hierarchy.

No workstream should proceed to production merely because a generated sample appears finished.

## First-cycle completion criteria

The first execution cycle is complete when:

- Operating-control documents and first cards exist.
- The confirmed website slice is implemented and browser-verified.
- Identity and visiting-card brief is ready for founder review.
- Storybook research plan is ready for evidence collection and supplier outreach.
- Story-to-distribution workflow is ready for one approved pilot.
- Remaining decisions are visible, owned, and gated rather than hidden inside drafts.
